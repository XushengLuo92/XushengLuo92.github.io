Choosing between task specifications

Task options

  • Option 1: move the yellow object to Target 5 and the blue object to Target 4 or 6, in any order.
  • Option 2: move the yellow object to Target 5 while avoiding the red region, then move the blue object to Target 1 or 5.

Building-wide service tasks

Six robots share an office building, coordinating service tasks with hierarchical temporal logic. Multiple tasks progress in parallel while the robots respect ordering, routing, and privacy constraints.

Tasks & constraints

  • Garbage collection: take the full paper bin from desk d5 to disposal area g, avoiding the public area while it is full. Then return an empty bin to d5.
  • Document distribution: deliver printed copies to desks d10, d7, and d5. Avoid public areas while carrying the documents.
  • Room photography: take photos in meeting rooms m1, m4, and m6. Keep the camera off outside meeting rooms.
  • Desk-to-desk delivery: move a document from d5 to d3 without passing through public areas.
  • Visitor guidance: escort the person waiting at desk d11 to meeting room m6.
