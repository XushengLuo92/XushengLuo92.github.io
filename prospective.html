02 / GET STARTED

Tell me what
excites you.

Prospective students and visitors can use the same lab interest form.

  1. Find the connection.

    Look through the lab’s research and recent papers. Tell me which questions interest you and how your background connects to them.

  2. Introduce yourself.

    Use the lab interest form to share your background and intended start term. A short, specific introduction is helpful.

  3. Apply to your graduate program.

    For M.S. and Ph.D. admission, complete the official NC State MAE application process. You may name Xusheng Luo and the IRTA Lab as your advisor and lab of interest. The lab interest form does not replace the university application.

Can’t access the form? Send an email. ＋

Email xluo28@ncsu.edu if you cannot access the form or upload files. Use this subject line:

Prospective Student (B.S./M.S./Ph.D.) — [Your Name]

Please include:

  • Your CV or résumé
  • Your transcript, if available
  • Your research interests and why the IRTA Lab is a good fit
  • One or two projects, papers, or code samples, if available
  • Your intended start term: Spring 2027 or Fall 2027

I review inquiries closely related to the lab’s work, but may not be able to reply to every message.

03 / LAB CULTURE

Serious research.
Shared progress.

I’m building a collaborative, rigorous, and supportive research environment. Students are encouraged to develop independent ideas, publish thoughtful work, build real systems, and collaborate across disciplines.

My mentoring focuses on technical depth alongside research communication, writing, presentations, collaboration, and long-term career planning—whether your next step is academia, industry, or a national lab.

For students exploring research, curiosity and initiative are a good starting point. Graduate applicants should also review the research or project experience requirements for their chosen degree.
