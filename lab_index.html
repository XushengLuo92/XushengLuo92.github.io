What we work on

Research areas

Five interconnected pillars spanning perception, reasoning, safety, and human–robot interaction.

Intelligent robotics

Developing robots capable of perception, reasoning, learning, and adaptive decision-making in complex environments.

Autonomous systems

Designing scalable autonomy for robotic and cyber-physical systems operating in dynamic and uncertain real-world settings.

Trustworthy autonomy

Advancing safe, reliable, explainable, and verifiable autonomy for learning-enabled systems.

Planning and control

Integrating planning, optimization, and control for intelligent autonomous behavior across multiple levels of decision-making.

Human-centered AI & robotics

Creating autonomous systems that can safely and effectively interact with humans and society.

Interested in our work? We publish in top robotics and control venues including ICRA, IROS, L-CSS, and IEEE T-RO.
