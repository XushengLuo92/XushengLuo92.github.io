XUSHENG LUO / ROBOTICS RESEARCHER

Intelligent robots.
Trustworthy by design.

Bridging mathematical rigor and machine intelligence to build robots that reason, collaborate, and act safely in the real world.

I’m Xusheng Luo, a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at North Carolina State University, where I direct the Intelligent Robotics and Trustworthy Autonomy (IRTA) Lab.

Previously, I worked with Changliu Liu at Carnegie Mellon’s Intelligent Control Lab. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Duke University in December 2020, advised by Michael M. Zavlanos, and my B.S. and M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2015 and 2017.
